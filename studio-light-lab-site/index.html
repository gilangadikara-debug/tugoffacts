About this simulator

Studio Light Lab is a free, browser-based studio lighting simulator for learning portrait and product photography. Place up to eight lights with modifiers such as softboxes, octaboxes, beauty dishes, umbrellas, strip boxes, grids, snoots and fresnels, add colour gels, and watch the result on a real 3D head scan, posable male and female figures, or still-life sets. A built-in light meter reports f-stops, exposure and lighting ratios, and the patterns menu loads classic setups like Rembrandt, loop, butterfly, split, clamshell and high key.

Studio Light Lab adalah simulator pencahayaan studio gratis berbasis peramban untuk belajar fotografi potret dan produk. Atur hingga delapan lampu dengan softbox, octabox, beauty dish, payung, strip box, grid, snoot, dan fresnel, tambahkan gel warna, lalu lihat hasilnya pada pindaian kepala 3D, figur pria dan wanita yang dapat diatur posenya, atau benda mati. Light meter bawaan menampilkan bukaan diafragma, eksposur, dan rasio pencahayaan.
