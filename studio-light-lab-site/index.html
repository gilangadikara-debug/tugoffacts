About this simulator

Studio Light Lab is a free, browser-based studio lighting simulator for learning portrait, product, food and fine-art photography. Place up to eight lights with softboxes, octaboxes, beauty dishes, umbrellas, strip boxes, grids, snoots and fresnels, add colour gels, and light 3D-scanned male and female models in portrait or full-length framing, a marble statue (the Laocoön Group), a cat, a burger, an 18% grey ball and still-life objects. A built-in light meter reports f-stops, exposure and lighting ratios, and the patterns menu loads classic setups like Rembrandt, loop, butterfly, split, clamshell and high key.

Studio Light Lab adalah simulator pencahayaan studio gratis berbasis peramban untuk belajar fotografi potret, produk, makanan, dan seni. Atur hingga delapan lampu dengan softbox, octabox, beauty dish, payung, strip box, grid, snoot, dan fresnel, tambahkan gel warna, lalu terangi model pria dan wanita hasil pindaian 3D, patung marmer (Laocoön), kucing, burger, bola abu-abu 18%, dan benda mati. Light meter bawaan menampilkan bukaan diafragma, eksposur, dan rasio pencahayaan.
